# Agtron

> Source: https://www.coffeereview.com/roast-definitions/

## Overview

Degree or darkness of roast dramatically affects a coffee’s flavor profile, as does how
the coffee has been brought to a given roast level: quickly with high temperatures,
slowly with low temperatures, etc.

No single degree of roast is necessarily better than another. Preferences in roast vary widely, influenced by tradition, brewing style (coffee intended for pour-over or batch brewing is often roasted lighter than coffee intended for espresso or French press). Today, the best roasters seek to hit what, to their palates, is the “sweet spot” for each green coffee, the point at which the structure of the coffee is most balanced and the aromas and flavors most complete and complex.

## Agtron readings

The Agtron is a spectrophotometer which uses infrared light to determine roast degree.
The higher the Agtron number, the lighter the roast.

A coffee’s degree of roast can be measured with some precision through the use of a specially modified spectrophotometer called an Agtron. We use the M-Basic or “Gourmet” Agtron scale, and for each coffee reviewed, we present readings of both the whole beans before grinding (the number preceding the slash) and the same beans after grinding (the number after the slash). For example, a reading of 55/68 would describe a coffee with an external, whole-bean M-Basic reading of 55 and a ground reading of 68.

| Roast | Agtron | Characteristics |
| Light | >80 | Light brown to cinnamon color |
| Medium - Light | 70 - 80 | Moderately light brown color. Bright, sweet acidity, green coffee distinctions clear |
| Medium | 60 - 70 | Medium brown color. Balanced acidity, fuller body, green coffee distinctions still apparent |
| Medium - Dark | 50 - 60 | Rich brown color. Fruit turns chocolatey, hints of roasty bittersweetnes. Muted aciditiy. |
| Dark | 40 - 50 | Deep brownish/black color. Richly to sharply bittersweet, scorched-wood roast notes prominent. |
| Very Dark | 25 - 40 | Black surface covered brightly with oil. Bitter/bittersweet tones dominate. Body things, green coffee distinctions fully muted. |
| Extreme Dark | < 25 | Black, shiny surface. Burned bitter tones dominate |
